- **Invited:** 10 testers by email (Bcc), for about two weeks. All 10 are listed as Google OAuth test
  users (Testing mode admits only them). The pilot team was created by Karim (the supervisor), code in
  the email. Caps unchanged: $0.50 a person a day, $5 a day, $50 in all (two weeks at the per-person
  cap could reach about $70: watch the spend before raising it).
- **The email asks for:** the watch day and night, the morning check-in and the evening question
  (one tap each), a few minutes of use a day, thumbs on answers and replies by email. Every feature
  is open; the daily limit is mentioned without figures.
- **First day:** one sign-up, approved; no tester has connected Google yet. Sign-ups are checked with
  `python -m app.auth pending` over `railway ssh`; Google connections in the API's HTTP logs.
- **Checked before sending:** the privacy test (below), every route behind a sign-in except sign-up,
  login, logout and Google's callback (single use, 10 minutes, tied to the person), a pending account
  reaching only its own status, the first sync starting on Google's callback (24 connect tests),
  the guide and pull to refresh in a browser.
- **Not yet measured:** how long a real first sync takes (estimated: under a minute for the newest
  months, a few minutes for years of history). Read it from the first tester's connection.

## Results, and which can be quoted as final accuracy

A result is **final** when it comes from questions or cases nothing was changed for after they were
seen. A **development** result comes from a set we read and then fixed the agent against: it shows
the fixes work, not how good the agent is on new questions. A **regression** check reruns a fixed
set to show nothing broke.

### How to say it in the report

- Lead with the final results: PHIA batch 2 (97.0%, and the comparison with the baselines) and its
  check against PHIA's own published key (A1, 95.7%), the
  router (98.7%), food matching (22 / 25), RAG (0.94 recall@3, with its caveat).
- Report the verifier as a component test that shows what it catches and that it rejects nothing
  true, not as an accuracy score.
- Say plainly that a one-line pandas expression beats every language system at pure arithmetic
  (99.7%), and what Patterns does that it does not: answers in words, checks every claim, handles
  every other kind of question.
- Times in PHIA batches 1 and 2 include the grader's reading call and are not compared.
